# Roadmap and Gates

## Shared enabler - Growth and Revenue Telemetry

- Outcome: connect evaluation, product success, CRM, billing, and support cost.
- Gate: stable account, organization, deal, approval, export, conversion, environment,
  CRM, and billing fields are queryable end to end.

## 1. Guided Evaluation and Sage Activation

- Revenue: evaluation-to-paid conversion and lower onboarding cost.
- Slice: goal selection, disposable workspace, sample deal, controlled model
  compatibility, persistent checklist, contextual Sage, handoff, and conversion.
- Gate: Cactus 2.0 meaningful-action conversion improves from 27.7% to at least 40%
  without worsening trust or error guardrails.

## 2. Trust Center

- Revenue: conversion, retention, and enterprise win rate.
- Slice: material T-12/rent-roll exception queue, evidence, approval, model impact,
  and audit.
- Gate: material review time improves at least 30% with no increase in escaped
  material errors and 10 beta accounts completing approval.

## 3. Instant Deal Screen

- Revenue: new-logo conversion, repeat usage, and deal throughput.
- Slice: intake, saved criteria, explainable pass/fail/uncertain states, and promotion
  to underwriting.
- Gate: faster package-to-decision time, repeat screens per account, and operational
  false-negative review.

## 4. Market Intelligence and Memory

- Revenue: retention, expansion, and differentiated paid value.
- Slice: evidence-backed rent comps, accept/reject, assumption attachment, and private
  comp reuse.
- Gate: faster approved comp selection and repeated reuse before source or asset-class
  expansion.

## 5. Model Studio

- Revenue: enterprise ACV, pilot conversion, and expansion.
- Slice: one asset-class workbook, mappings, supported formulas, unsupported-construct
  detection, versioned templates, and tie-out.
- Gate: at least 80% of selected pilot workbooks tie out within tolerance with no
  silent unsupported failures.

## 6. Enterprise Workflow

- Revenue: higher ACV, multi-user deployment, and expansion.
- Slice: roles, audit, SSO diagnostics, one pipeline-selected connector, async jobs,
  status, and webhooks.
- Gate: qualified design partners move from champion-only use to measurable multi-user
  production.

## 7. Scenario Lab

- Revenue: engagement, retention, and seat expansion.
- Slice: five bounded assumption changes, diff preview, confirmation, immutable base
  case, comparison, save, and lineage.
- Gate: at least 70% of validated common requests complete without analyst help and
  within agreed quality limits.

## 8. Decision Packages

- Revenue: workflow completion, collaboration, and expansion.
- Slice: validate 20 concierge packages, then productize one source-linked IC template.
- Gate: reduce approved-underwriting-to-shared-package time by at least 50% with zero
  unsupported claims.

## 9. Development Underwriter

- Revenue: new segment ARR.
- Slice: one multifamily ground-up archetype with cost stack, draw, lease-up,
  permissions, and formula-aware export.
- Gate: 5-8 paid or committed partners approve the model without offline rebuild.

## 10. Commercial Lease Intelligence

- Revenue: new asset-class ARR.
- Slice: one asset class and lease type with amendment chain, critical terms,
  discrepancy review, rollover schedule, and handoff.
- Gate: segment selected from qualified pipeline, at least 500 leases benchmarked,
  accuracy threshold defined, and paid commitments secured.

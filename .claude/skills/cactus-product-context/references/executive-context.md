# Executive Context

## Snapshot

- Evidence date: 2026-07-27.
- Qualitative source: 946 unique external Sybill meetings.
- Corpus scale: approximately 5.72 million words and 113,688 normalized turns.
- Behavioral sources: Cactus 1.0 and Cactus 2.0 PostHog projects.
- Public edition: identities, verbatim quotes, private links, and account-level data
  are excluded.

## Product promise

Turn a messy real-estate deal package into a defensible, reusable decision while
preserving the customer's model, evidence, and judgment.

## Strategic loop

1. Let a qualified prospect reach credible value before paying.
2. Make extracted inputs and model effects trustworthy enough to approve.
3. Restore repeat screening and market-intelligence habits.
4. Preserve Excel models and controls for enterprise adoption.
5. Add governance, scenario control, collaboration, and decision outputs.
6. Enter development and commercial segments only through narrow paid gates.

## Product-version lens

- Cactus 1.0 identifies proven jobs and usage intensity.
- Cactus 2.0 tests whether the refactor improves success, trust, and scalability.
- Cross-version evidence tests preservation and migration, not simple parity.

Evaluate the refactor through four questions:

1. Preservation: were high-value Cactus 1.0 jobs carried forward?
2. Improvement: are they easier to discover, complete, verify, and repeat?
3. Scale: can more accounts succeed with less manual onboarding and support?
4. Monetization: do improvements increase conversion, retention, expansion, or ACV?

## Current strategic conclusion

Build Guided Evaluation, Trust Center, and Instant Deal Screen as one conversion
system. The prospect should experience value, verify the result, and establish a
repeat habit. Build telemetry first so downstream revenue effects are measurable.

Higher-ACV Model Studio and Enterprise Workflow remain important but depend on
supported technical boundaries, model tie-out, identity, roles, audit, and deployment
measurement.

## Boundaries

Do not build an unlimited anonymous free trial, a generic ungoverned Sage chatbot,
arbitrary VBA support, a replacement CRM, construction project management, or full
ARGUS parity without paid evidence and explicit gates.

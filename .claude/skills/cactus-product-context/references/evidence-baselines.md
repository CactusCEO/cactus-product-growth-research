# Evidence Baselines

## Cactus 1.0

- External identified monthly activity: 118 users in July 2025 and 24 in partial
  July 2026. Treat the decline as migration-confounded.
- Quick Analysis: 100,593 pageviews from 215 external identified users over the
  trailing twelve months, or 467.9 views per user.
- Interpretation: screening was a high-intensity legacy habit that Cactus 2.0 must
  deliberately preserve and improve.

## Cactus 2.0

- External identified monthly activity: 33 users in December 2025, 64 in June 2026,
  and 52 in partial July 2026.
- Signup to meaningful action within 14 days: 91 of 329 test-filtered users, or
  27.7%. Meaningful action means chat submission, extraction start, or template
  creation.
- Mature identified-user retention: 43.3% in week 1 and 18.6% in week 4, with small
  cohorts.
- Extraction completion within one day: 29 of 34 users, or 85.3%; median 40.8
  seconds.
- Confidence verification: 0.766 average confidence, 948.8 source matches, and
  1,362.1 verified facts per completion across 710 completion events.
- Market Analysis: 1,415 submitted messages from 85 external users across 50
  organizations.
- Excel extraction: 327 successes from 342 completions, or 95.6%, across 31 users;
  successful runs averaged 147.6 seconds. Event success does not prove workbook
  tie-out.
- Assistant response completion: 90 of 106 test-filtered submitters received a
  completion within ten minutes; median 2.1 seconds. Scenario correctness is
  unmeasured.
- Organization invitations: 36 sent across 18 organizations and 23 accepted across
  15 organizations. Sent and accepted events are not joined by invite.
- Rent-roll completion: 2 of 4 triggering users within 30 minutes; too small for a
  broad commercial decision.

## Interpretation limits

- The PostHog rows use different populations and windows; they are not one funnel.
- July 2026 is partial through July 27.
- Person properties reflect event-time values.
- Some Cactus 2.0 server events have inconsistent environment labels.
- Approval, export, paid conversion, scenario creation, and several decision outcomes
  remain under-instrumented.
- Transcript counts describe prevalence in recorded conversations, not market-share
  estimates or causal revenue impact.
